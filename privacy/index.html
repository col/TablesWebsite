Privacy

Private by design.

Tables is built so there's nothing to collect in the first place. This page is written in plain language — no legal fog.

Last updated 24 July 2026

The short version

No account, no sign-up, no tracking. Everything your child does stays on their device. We don't have servers that store your data because we never collect it.

What we collect

Nothing that leaves the device. Tables works entirely offline. There's no account to create, no email to enter, and no analytics or advertising SDKs inside the app.

Practice data

Your child's scores, best runs, and mastery grid are saved only on the device, so progress is there next time. This information is never uploaded and we have no way to see it.

The microphone

Voice answering is not in the current version. If it ships, it will process speech on the device to check the answer — audio is never recorded, stored, or sent anywhere, and you'll be asked before the microphone is ever used.

No third parties

Tables contains no ad networks, no trackers, and no social logins. Nothing about your child is shared with or sold to anyone, because none of it exists outside the device.

Children's privacy

Tables is made for children and holds no personal data at all. Because there are no accounts and nothing is collected, there is nothing to consent to, export, or delete beyond simply deleting the app.

Changes to this page

If a future update ever changes how data is handled, we'll update this page and note it in the App Store release notes before the change takes effect.

Questions about privacy? Email colin@challengr.io and a real person will reply.
